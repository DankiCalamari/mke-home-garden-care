Who we are

Your local one-stop property care team

MKE Home & Garden Care is a local Mildura business built on a simple idea: give homeowners and businesses one call to cover everything their property needs.

We bring together garden care, cleaning and trades under one roof. That means no more coordinating multiple contractors, no dropped balls, and one consistent quality standard across every job — whether it's a lawn, a set of gutters, a stack of solar panels, or a full renovation.

Our goal is to take all the worry out of engaging trades, so you get more time and a better-looking property with less effort.
